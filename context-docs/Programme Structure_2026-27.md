RETURN-AND-REPLACE (R&R) POLICY FOR PGP STUDENTS:

PURPOSE:  The  term  'Return-and-  Replace'  may  be  defined  as  an  opportunity  to  'return'  to  School  in  the  following  academic  year  to  'replace'  courses  in  which  a

student may have under-performed, with better grades in the same course or another course that the student may choose.

Please  note:  'Replace'  refers  to  replacing  course  grades  for  the  purpose  of  CGPA  calculation  and  determining  whether  student  has  met  graduation  requirements.  However,  the  revised

grades of the course(s) (which is/are being replaced) will be reported on the final transcript. Only credit courses will be considered for R&R. Pass/Fail courses will not be available for R&R.

Objective of the R&R policy:

The objective is to provide an opportunity to students to Graduate in the following year.

This would apply to students who have not been able to graduate either because they have not completed their requirement of 32 successful credits (Including 4 block week credits and

ELP), or their CGPA is less that the minimum requirement of 2.5, but equal to or more than 2.20. The student should have essentially completed at least 28 successful credits,

i.e., got an F or Fail grade in not more than four credits, to avail of this policy.

The policy is applicable only to students who have NO Honour Code violations in any course during their original academic year.
At the time of application of the policy, the student cannot choose to change Core credits to Pass/Fail.

Validity:

Students are eligible for R&R options only in the academic year immediately following the year of their original admission. In case of students returning to the programme after being on

'Leave of Absence' (LoA), the students are eligible to avail R&R options with the immediate next batch of the programme.

For e.g., a student, who was originally from the Class of 2025 and had returned to complete the programme with the Class of 2026 after LoA but did not meet the necessary graduation

requirements at that time, may avail of this policy with the Class of 2025 only, and not after that. Therefore, the student can avail of R&R only with the batch immediately following the

batch with which s/he completed all the course work.

Format for replacing courses:

Courses for R&R could be offered in three formats:

In-person In-class
Completely virtual classes (applicable where the programme provides for virtual or online classes).
Mixed classes of virtual and In Class – a Hybrid Model (applicable where the programme provides for Hybrid classes)

If the course is offered in hybrid format, the student may choose to attend the course online completely. Please note: It will not be possible for her/him to take two different courses at

different campuses in the same term even if a Hybrid format is offered.

If the course is being offered on campus and in-person only, it will be mandatory for the student to attend classes on campus only. Under the R&R Policy a student can choose to take

courses on the other campus, for e.g., A Hyderabad student may choose to avail courses at Mohali and vice versa.

No. of courses permitted under R&R:

A student can take up to FOUR credits when s/he returns. S/he must pay the proportionate tuition fee applicable for the for each credit and any appropriate accommodation charges at

rates in effect at the time of returning to take courses.

Parameters for availing R&R:

For a student who did not complete 32 successful credits but completed at least 28 successful credits or had a CGPA less than 2.50 but more than 2.20, the student can retake courses

that s/he has already taken or take fresh courses not taken before to meet the Graduation requirements, subject to the following:

For a student whose original CGPA was below 2.50, new grades will be reported on the final transcript but CGPA on the final transcript will be reported as 2.50 (limit) only even if
the performance has improved with R&R, and the computed CGPA with the new grades is greater than or equal to 2.50.
For a student who did not complete 32 successful credits but had CGPA greater than or equal to 2.50, new grades will be reported on the final transcript but CGPA on the final
transcript will not increase from the original CGPA.
For a student who did not complete 32 successful credits and had a CGPA less than 2.50, policy applied will be based on CGPA being less than 2.50 (described in the first bullet
point above) but they would still have to complete 32 successful credits in addition to having a computed CGPA greater than or equal to 2.50. The final CGPA however will show as
2.50 only on the transcript.
The student who avails R&R will not be eligible for any academic award in either of the year of study

Exceptions:

No exceptions will be made for students who wish to avail the R&R Policy. All the above conditions will be applicable.

Fee:

Each course will have a fixed fee. The students will pay for all the courses opted by him/her.

The fee for the "replaced" course will be paid by the student based on the fee structure of the year of availing the R&R policy. The student will also pay applicable accommodation charges

for campus stay as required to complete the course/s.

Policy for Withdrawal from the Programme

If  a  student  wants  to  withdraw  from  the  Programme,  the  student  must  apply  a  formal  request  on  the  Registrar's  office  portal  (Link)  with  the  reason  and  supporting  documents  if

applicable. The Registrar's office will process the request and communicate to the student regarding the approval. The fee refund will be applicable as per the refund policy of the school.

Policy for Leave of Absence from the Programme

The student can apply for leave of absence for the academic year either for personal or academic reasons. S/he must apply a formal request to the Registrar's office portal (Link). The

School can grant leave of absence, once the HoD, Registrar's office approves it. The student can rejoin the programme within the next two subsequent years from the term that S/he has

left the programme and complete the programme requirements. The student will have to pay the tuition fees and living expenses applicable at the time when they rejoin the program. The

student is expected to attend classes while the request is in process until the approval is communicated by Registrar's office.

Certificate/Transcripts

The student will receive a Digitally Certified Certificate and Transcript on successful completion of the Programme at the end of the year after the Graduation Day and after meeting all

financial obligations with the

School. Registrar's Office will issue the Certificate and Transcript through "Parchment" website. Parchment is a secure online service that allow organisations to issue secure digital records

online, so that the subjects of those records can access and share them on the web with third parties. This eliminates the hassle associated with having to produce original paper

documents.

Student will receive an email (on their ISB alum id) with login credentials. Student can view, print and share the document. This facility will help the student having Life-long access to the

document and can be accessed from any part of the world.

The hard copy of the original certificate will be issued at the time of graduation.

a.      Application Fee: This fee, payable at the time of submission of completed application form for the PGP, is non-refundable.

b.     Admission Fee: - Admission fee is partially refundable subject to following term and conditions:

REFUND POLICY

1. A sum of Rs. 50,000 out of the admission fee paid shall be forfeited irrespective of when the candidate withdraws.
2. If the candidate withdraws on or before 90 days before the start of the programme, Admission fee less Rs. 50,000 shall be refunded.
3. If the candidate withdraws on or before 60 days before the start of the programme, Admission fee less Rs. 1,00,000 shall be refunded.
4. If the candidate withdraws on or before 30 days before the start of the programme, admission fee collected less Rs. 1,50,000 shall be refunded.
5. No refunds shall be allowed for withdrawal from programme if the withdrawal is communicated less than 30 days prior to the commencement of the programme.
6.  No refunds shall be allowed for the deferral candidate if the withdrawal from the programme is not in the year in which he was supposed to join the program as per the offer letter.

The same rule shall be applicable with respect to the class to which the original offer of admission was made to the candidate.

Note: -The registration day is considered as the start date of the programme for this purpose.

c.      Commitment Fee: Commitment fee charged to the student is non-refundable.

d. Tuition Fees: The tuition fee paid at the time of enrolment into the PGP class is partly refundable in case the student withdraws from the programme as mentioned below.

1. If the student pays the tuition fees as due and then withdraws from the programme before the date of registration, 100% of tuition fees shall be refunded.
2. If the student withdraws during the orientation week but before the commencement of Term-1, 10% of the tuition fees payable for terms 1-4 shall be forfeited and the balance

amount shall be refunded.

3. If the student withdraws during the first 4 terms, 100 % of the tuition fees proportionately for terms already commenced and 50% of the fees relating to the Terms that have not

yet commenced in the first four terms shall be forfeited. The balance amount along with 100% of fees for remaining terms/block weeks (if already paid) shall be refunded.

4. If the student withdraws during term 5 or later, 100% of the tuition fees for terms and block weeks already commenced and 50% of the fees relating to terms and block weeks not
yet commenced shall be forfeited and the balance amount shall be refunded. For this purpose, the tuition fees for one block week shall be considered to be one-fourth of the tuition
fees of a regular term.

5. Fee waiver if any granted to the student shall be withdrawn if the student withdraws from the programme / dismissed / expelled from the programme. A student who is dismissed /

expelled from the programme, for disciplinary reasons or an Honour Code violation shall not be entitled to any refund.

d.     Accommodation Expenses: In case of vacation of the accommodation on account of withdrawal from the programme, proceeding on exchange programme, expulsion or dismissal

of the student from the programme, proportionate charges for the remaining calendar months of the programme from the date of vacation of the accommodation shall be refunded.

e.            Security  Deposit:  This  deposit,  paid  by  the  student  at  the  time  of  registration  to  the  PGP,  is  refundable  to  the  student  at  the  time  of  exit  (i.e.  either  on  completion  of  the

programme or on withdrawal, dismissal or expulsion) from the PGP subject to recoveries, if any (such as amounts outstanding telephone, dining, electricity, damages caused to the assets

etc. by the student).

Refund due to the student in accordance with the above policy shall be made within ten working days of completion of exit formalities by the student. The amount payable shall be along

with applicable GST as per rules at the time of refund. If the student has taken loan from the bank, then the amount refundable shall be remitted to the bank directly except in case where

the bank has issued an NOC for payment to the student.
