Q1. How many courses can I bid for during Block Week?

Academic Affairs Office

Ans: You can bid for only one course (maximum 1, minimum 0) during the entire process
for each Block Week.
Q2. Is it mandatory to participate in Block Week Bidding?

Ans: No. Participation is optional if you can meet your 19-credit graduation requirement
(including ELP) through other terms.

Q6. Can I drop my course after bidding?

Ans: Yes, during Phase 2 – Drop Phase, you can drop tentatively registered or waitlisted
courses.
Q7. How does the waitlist work?

Ans: • Waitlists do not carry forward beyond Phase 2 for Block weeks.
• After Phase 2, all waitlists are dissolved.
• If you were waitlisted and dropped your bid, you can re-bid during Phase 4.
Q8. What is Phase 4 – Add Phase?

Ans: 3 hours bidding window where students without tentative registration can bid for
available seats (visible during this phase). Students already with tentative registration
cannot participate. Students who have participated in Phase-1 are only eligible to
participate in Phase-4.
Q9. Can I change my grading option to Pass/Fail?

Ans: Yes, in the Pass/Fail Conversion Round, you can convert eligible tentatively registered
courses to Pass/Fail.
Q10. What is the Counselling Round?

Ans: An offline process for students without any registration after Phase 4. Preferences are
collected via an online form, and seats are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.
Q11. Will the Counselling Round be open for all students?

Ans: No, the Counselling Round will be open only for the students who participated in the
bidding process of the Block Week.
Q12. How many sections does each course have?

Ans: Each course is limited to maximum of two sections only.
