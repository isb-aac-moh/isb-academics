PHASE 1

Add — Initial Sealed Bidding

First Saturday of the prior term, 10:00h → Monday 08:00h, Week 2 of the prior term · Duration: 2 days

Your only entry point into the cycle. You submit sealed bids on faculty-course combinations without seeing anyone else's numbers. Miss this and Phase 4 or the offline counselling round are your only ways back in.

Q

How many courses can I bid on, and how many bid points do I have?

You have 4,500 points total. Term Max Credits in Term 4 is 4 (min 3), so you can hold bids across as many courses as you like up to that credit ceiling. Minimum bid per course is 100 points.

The system automatically reserves 100 points per remaining required credit, so you can't accidentally bid yourself out of graduation requirements.

Q

What happens if two students place the same bid?

Your tiebreaker score — a random number assigned and shared during Phase 1 — decides it. Lower score wins priority. It's fixed for the term once assigned.

Q

Can I edit a bid after submitting it?

Yes, any time before the round closes. Bids auto-save; there's no manual submit button. When the countdown hits zero, whatever's in your Bid Consideration Set locks in.

Registering 4 courses instead of the minimum 3 — perfectly fine, since Term 4's max is 4 credits. Nothing stops you from bidding the ceiling in Phase 1 and deciding later which one to keep or drop.

PHASE 2

Pre-Sample Drop

Friday 10:00h → Saturday 10:00h, Week 2 (prior term) · Duration: 1 day

Still before the term has started — no classes exist yet. You get your first look at indicative clearing prices (ICP1) and can rebalance by dropping, but you cannot add or increase bids.

Q

Can I attend a class and then decide here?

No — Phase 2 runs entirely within the prior term, before Term 4 classes begin. There's nothing to attend yet. This phase is for reacting to indicative clearing prices, not classroom experience.

Classes haven't started
Q

What can I actually do here?

Drop any tentatively registered or waitlisted course — points refund post-processing. You cannot place new bids or raise existing ones. Real-time bid fluctuations aren't shown, only the indicative price.

Q

What if ASA drops a course I bid on?

Points are refunded immediately. That course is gone for good — once dropped from the offerings, it can't be added back.

PHASE 3

Post-Sample Drop

Term Days 1–2, until 2 hours after the last class on Day 2 (~Tuesday) · Duration: ~2 days

Classes have actually started. This is the phase built for exactly the "attend first, then decide" approach — you can sit in a session and drop what doesn't work before locking in.

Q

I registered 4 courses in Phase 1. I want to attend a class before deciding which to drop — and I'm not adding anything new. Does that work?

Yes. This is precisely what Phase 3 is for. You attend the first session(s), then use Drop next to whichever course you no longer want. Points refund post-processing, and you're left with your remaining registered courses — no need to touch Phase 4 at all.

Fits your case exactly
Q

If I attend a class, drop that course, then add a different one later in Phase 4 — do I lose that attended class?

Not automatically lost, but not automatically carried either. The deck states: attendance from a dropped course is transferable to the first new course added as a replacement — but this transfer is done offline via the LMS, not by the system itself.

So the mechanism exists, but it requires manual processing. If you drop in Phase 3 and later add a replacement in Phase 4, follow up with ASA/LMS to confirm the transfer actually posted.

Manual, offline step — confirm with ASA
Q

Can I still see courses I dropped in earlier phases?

Yes — the Overview shows Pre-Enrolled Courses, Tentatively Registered/Waitlisted Courses, and Dropped Courses, so your full history through Phase 3 stays visible.

Your exact scenario — attend, then drop, no new add:

  • Phase 1: bid and get tentatively registered for 4 courses (Term 4 max is 4)
  • Term begins; Days 1–2 classes run
  • You attend one or more of the 4 classes
  • Phase 3 window is open (until 2 hrs after last class, Day 2)
  • Click Drop on the course you don't want — confirm the prompt
  • Bid points refund post Phase 3 processing
  • You're left with 3 courses — meets the Term 4 minimum elective credit (3)
  • No add needed, no attendance-transfer question arises
PHASE 4

Post-Sampling Add — Final Add

Term Day 3 (Wednesday) · 60-minute window

Your last shot at adding anything. Fresh sealed bids only — nothing carries over automatically from earlier phases except waitlist priority.

Q

Do I have to participate in Phase 4?

Only if you want to add a course. If you're happy with what survived Phase 3, you can skip Phase 4 entirely — there's no penalty for sitting it out if you're not adding anything.

Q

I was waitlisted earlier — does that carry in?

Yes, students already on a waitlist from earlier phases get priority. But note: a new, independent waitlist forms in Phase 4, separate from the one carried from Phase 1.

Q

What if I missed Phase 1 entirely — can Phase 4 fix that?

No. The deck is explicit: students who didn't participate in Phase 1 cannot participate in Phase 4 either. Your only path is the offline Phase 6 counselling round.

No Phase 1 → no Phase 4
PHASE 5

Final Drop

Term Day 3 (Wednesday), 1 hour after Phase 4 concludes · 60-minute window

The very last chance to drop anything. After this closes, your course list for the term is locked — no further adds, no further drops.

Q

Can I drop below my minimum required credits here?

No — the system blocks any drop that would take you below the term's minimum elective credit requirement, with an on-screen error explaining exactly that.

Q

Does attendance transfer apply in Phase 5 too?

The deck doesn't state this for Phase 5 — the attendance-transfer language is specific to Phase 3. Since Phase 5 has no add option (it's drop-only), there's no "replacement course" for attendance to transfer to anyway.

Drop-only round — no add, no transfer
Q

What happens to waitlists after Phase 5?

All waitlists dissolve once Phase 5 closes.

PHASE 6

Counselling Round

Term Day 3 (Wednesday), offline · Same-day

An offline safety net, not a bidding round. Run by ASA directly for students still short of requirements after everything else has closed.

Q

Who is this for?

Students with fewer than the minimum required term courses, or anyone still short of graduation requirements after Phase 5. ASA assists directly wherever seats remain.

Q

Can I drop a course allotted here?

No — courses allocated in Phase 6 cannot be dropped.

Final and non-reversible
Q

What price do I pay for a Phase 6 course?

Clearing Price 2 (from Phase 4) is applied to all courses registered through this round.
